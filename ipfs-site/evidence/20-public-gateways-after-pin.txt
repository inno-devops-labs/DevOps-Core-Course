It also surfaces a real 2026 trend worth noting in the Centralized vs
Decentralized analysis (4EVERLAND.md §7): the *number* of unauthenticated
public IPFS gateways has shrunk dramatically since 2023. Cloudflare,
Pinata, Fleek, NFT.Storage etc. have moved to authenticated/paid tiers,
expired their certs, or shut down outright. "Decentralized" today still
depends on a small number of well-funded gateway operators (Protocol
Labs' dweb.link backbone, 4EVERLAND, Storacha) -- which is itself an
argument for self-hosting an IPFS gateway when long-term availability
matters.
